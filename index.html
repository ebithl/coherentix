About Coherentix

Engineering depth, real-world delivery.

Founded by a collective of seasoned engineers, we operate at the intersection of embedded systems, cloud, data, and AI — building products that have to work in the real world, not just the demo.

Who we are

Coherentix Labs is a global technology company founded by a collective of seasoned engineers and consultants with over 150 years of combined experience. Our roots lie in deep product engineering expertise across embedded systems, software platforms, and hardware.

We bring together hardware and software proficiency in one team, and have delivered products across medical devices, industrial automation, networking infrastructure, and healthcare platforms.

At our core, we're engineers who understand not just technology, but the discipline of building reliable, scalable, and intelligent systems.

What we do

We help clients turn ideas into intelligent, compliant, market-ready products — for startups and enterprises alike — through engineering precision and deep domain understanding.

Our services span the full lifecycle, from concept and prototyping to regulatory-compliant deployment, particularly in medical and healthcare, where we navigate US FDA 510(k), IEC 60601, IEC 62304, and ISO 14971.

Beyond healthcare, we design connected solutions for industrial automation, automotive systems, and consumer electronics.
